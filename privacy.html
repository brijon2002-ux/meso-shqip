Privacy Policy — Learn Albanian

Last updated: [DD Month 2026]

This Privacy Policy explains how Learn Albanian App (“we”, “us”) handles information in connection with the Learn Albanian mobile application (the “App”). We have built the App to collect as little personal information as possible.

This is a plain-language template, not legal advice — review it against your actual data practices and, if in doubt, have it checked by a professional.

1. No account required

You do not need to create an account, sign in, or give us your name or email to use the App. Your learning progress (lessons completed, streaks, XP) is stored locally on your device.

2. Information processed when you make a purchase

The App offers a one-time in-app purchase to unlock all content. Purchases are handled by the app store (Google Play or Apple) and by our purchase-infrastructure provider, RevenueCat. When you buy or restore a purchase, the following may be processed:

  • Purchase and transaction history (what was bought, and whether it is active);
  • A randomly generated app-user identifier used to remember your purchase;
  • Device and technical identifiers (e.g. device model, operating-system version, country) used to deliver and validate the purchase.

We do not receive your card number or payment details — those are handled entirely by Google Play or Apple.

3. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the App sends you learning reminders (for example, daily streak reminders). These are scheduled on your device. You can turn them off at any time in your device settings. We do not use notifications to collect personal information.

4. How the information is used

  • To provide and unlock the content you purchase;
  • To restore your purchase across your devices;
  • To keep the App working, secure, and free of fraud;
  • To understand, in aggregate, how the App is used so we can improve it.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

5. Who we share it with

Information is shared only with service providers who help us run the App:

  • Google Play / Apple — process payments and app distribution (Google Privacy Policy, Apple Privacy Policy);
  • RevenueCat — manages in-app purchases and entitlements (RevenueCat Privacy Policy).

6. Data retention

Local progress remains on your device until you delete the App or clear its data. Purchase records are retained by our providers for as long as needed to honour your purchase and to meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

7. Children

The App is a general-audience educational tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under UK GDPR and EU GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information, or to object to or restrict its processing. Because the App requires no account, the simplest way to remove locally stored data is to delete the App. For purchase-related data, contact us using the details below and we will help.

9. International transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries outside your own, including the United States. Where required, appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your information.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows the latest version. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
